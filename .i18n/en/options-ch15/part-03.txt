@@ a533415b5d html
<strong>How it happens.</strong> The day before earnings, you buy 10 ATM straddles with 7 days to expiry (stock $100, IV 85%) for <span class="num">$9,383</span>; the implied expected move is about ±9.4%. You lose whether you guessed the direction right or wrong, because the actual move was smaller than the move the market had already priced in.
