@@ 667bd1fc0f html
<strong>Cash account</strong>: you pay for options in full. Selling a put requires setting aside the full amount in cash; for example, selling one 90 put for $40 ties up <span class="num">90 × 100 − 40 = $8,960</span>. <strong>Margin account</strong>: you can borrow to buy stock (Reg T initial margin is 50%), and you can trade spreads and sell naked.
