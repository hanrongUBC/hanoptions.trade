@@ 3b1efa4d51 html
The facts first. A US investment advisory firm specialized in selling options on commodity futures for its clients, mainly selling deep OTM options to collect premium. The appeal of this kind of strategy is its high win rate: most deep OTM options expire worthless. In mid-November 2018, natural gas futures surged within a very short time, while the firm’s client accounts held large numbers of short natural gas calls.
