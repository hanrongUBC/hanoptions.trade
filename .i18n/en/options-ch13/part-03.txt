Pin risk: closing right at the strike
@@ fbfd3b3095 html
On expiration day the stock closes at $100.10, and you are short 10 of the 100 calls. Whether you get exercised won’t be known until the weekend: holders can still decide after the close, based on after-hours prices. If you are assigned, you start Monday’s open short 1,000 shares; if Monday opens 3% higher, you lose <span class="num">1,000 × 100.10 × 3% = $3,003</span>. Spreads are no different: if at the close only the short leg is just in the money and the long leg expires worthless, you can be assigned with no protection. The rule is simple: <strong>before the close on expiration day, close every short leg within 1% of the current price</strong>.
@@ 67118f4bd3 html
