@@ fba4983ebf html

Where 1%–2% comes from
@@ 7252176b08 html
There's no rigorous theory behind this number; it comes from a simple trade-off. The long-run excess return of stocks over cash is roughly a few percentage points a year (an assumption; it varies a lot from period to period). Beyond 2%–3%, the insurance itself eats most of the edge stocks have over cash, and you'd be better off simply holding less stock.
