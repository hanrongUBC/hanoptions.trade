Three things that make VIX options different
@@ d76af6c5b2 html
<strong>The underlying is a future.</strong> The VIX index itself can’t be traded; VIX options are priced off the VIX future for the same expiration month. In calm periods the future usually sits above spot: with spot VIX at 15, the same-month future may be around 17, and whether a call is in or out of the money depends on the future.
@@ fba0faded5 html
<strong>The settlement price is a special opening quote.</strong> VIX options are European-style and cash-settled. On the morning of expiration (usually a Wednesday), a special quote (ticker VRO) is calculated from the opening prices of SPX options and used as the settlement price. It can be very different from the previous day’s closing VIX, and at the close the day before expiration you don’t know what the settlement price will be.
