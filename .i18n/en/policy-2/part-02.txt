Buy the stock or sell a put?
@@ 2f8b565391 html
A simple rule of thumb:
@@ 2fe58479bd html
For example: the stock is at $50 and IV is 45%. You sell a put that expires in 30 days with a strike of $47.5 and collect about $1.40 in premium. The probability of being assigned (buying the stock at $47.5) is about 36%. If the stock rises to $57.5 a month later, the person who bought the stock has made $7.5, while the put seller has made only $1.40. So selling puts suits underlyings where you can say, “If it falls, I'm happy to buy; if it rises, I won't mind missing it.”
