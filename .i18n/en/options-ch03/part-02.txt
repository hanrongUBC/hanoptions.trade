What happens when parity is violated
@@ 2f0f0c4d57 html
Suppose the call is quoted at 3.80 while the put is still 3.263. Sell the call, buy the put and buy the stock (a combination called a conversion): you pay out <span class="num">100 + 3.263 − 3.80 = 99.463</span>. At expiration, wherever the stock is, you sell it at 100: if it has risen, it is called away through the call; if it has fallen, you sell it with the put. Market makers and arbitrage programs would do this trade immediately, until prices moved back near parity. For individual traders, parity is useful for checking quotes and understanding positions; leave the arbitrage to the market makers.

@@ a7f20fed2a html
