<span class="num">5</span><span>Someone says “a covered call is safer than a cash-secured put.” Use parity to explain why the two carry the same risk, and name two factors that make them not quite the same in practice.</span>
@@ f43a7a5c80 html
Rearranging parity gives <span class="num">S − C = K·e^{−rT} − P</span>: owning stock and selling a call has exactly the same starting cost and payoff at expiration as holding cash equal to the present value of the strike and selling a put. Real-world differences: if the cash behind the short put earns no interest, you miss out on the interest on the strike; with American options, early assignment can come at different times; and who gets the dividend, the number of trades and the tax treatment also differ.
