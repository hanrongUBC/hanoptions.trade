<span class="num">2</span><span>The stock is at $100, the 105 put is quoted at 6.32 and the 95 call at 6.65 (both 30 days). What are the intrinsic value and time value of each? Why is their time value different?</span>
@@ 03b44e3cd1 html
105 put: intrinsic value 5.00, time value 1.32. 95 call: intrinsic value 5.00, time value 1.65. Both are $5 in the money; the difference comes from interest. The 95 call's time value ≈ the 95 put's price of 1.33 + interest of <span class="num">95 × (1 − e<sup>−0.04×30/365</sup>) = 0.31</span>; the 105 put's time value ≈ the 105 call's price of 1.66 − interest of 0.34. Interest pushes the time value of in-the-money calls up and that of in-the-money puts down.
