Start with Chapter 1

The whole course is about one idea

A stock has one dimension: up or down. An option has three. Every strategy is a different combination of the three, and every loss comes from a dimension you didn't notice.

DirectionDelta · Gamma

Where the underlying goes, and how fast. Chapter 4 explains it; Chapters 7 and 8 use it to choose a structure.

TimeTheta

Who pays and who collects as each day passes. Chapter 5 covers the shape of the decay; Chapter 8 covers how sellers make a living from it.

VolatilityVega · IV

The gap between the volatility the market expects and the volatility that actually happens. Chapters 6, 9 and 10 are built around it.

Three ways to read the course

New to options

Read in order. Chapters 1–3 lay the foundation; Chapters 4–6 are the most important part of the course, so don't skip them. Do each chapter's self-test before moving on.

Already trading options

Start with Chapter 4, Chapter 6, Chapter 13 and Chapter 15. Use the stress tests and the “five ways accounts die” to check your current account, then go back and fill in the other chapters.

Just looking something up

Go to the Appendix: more than a hundred terms, a quick-reference table and payoff diagrams for more than twenty structures, and templates you can copy as is.

The chapter structure of this course follows the outline of “US Options Trading” on emacrotrading.com; all text, cases, charts and numbers were written and calculated from scratch.

Examples use the Black-Scholes model by default: a 4% rate, no dividends, a 365-day year and 100 shares per contract. Any market data that has to be assumed is stated in the text. This course is for learning and is not investment advice.
